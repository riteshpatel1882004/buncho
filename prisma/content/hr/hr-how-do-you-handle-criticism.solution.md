**Expert answer**

> I try to understand the feedback rather than taking it personally. If someone points out a mistake in my work, I evaluate whether the feedback is valid and use it to improve.

> I believe constructive criticism is important, especially when you're starting your career.

**Tips to make it yours**

- Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- Pick a real example and say what you did, not what "we" did.
- End with what you learned.
